About

Notes I actually use to get through PGDA

Hi, I’m Courts. I’m doing the same PGDA/CTA year you are right now.

Before this, I tutored accounting all through my undergrad, and I completed the Bridging to CTA through Milpark with two distinctions and an average of over 70% across all four modules. That came from a lot of trial and error in how I studied, and these notes are what I wish I’d had from the start.

Each section is a standalone PDF: the principle, the standard or section of the Act, the calculation layout, and the traps that cost marks in tests. Everything is in plain language and readable on a laptop or printed. You don’t have to buy a whole module. If deferred tax is what’s keeping you up, buy deferred tax.

Outside of studying, you’ll usually find me at the pool coaching swimming to younger kids. Helping people and giving back has always mattered to me, and this is my way of doing that for students going through the same year I am.

FAQs

Questions, answered

The things students ask most about the notes, paying and downloads.

What format are the notes in?

Every section is a PDF. Open it on a laptop, tablet or phone, or print it — the layout holds up either way.

What's inside each section?

A comprehensive summary of the topic, with the core concepts and principles explained clearly and in full. Worked examples where the topic calls for them, with calculations and applications done step by step. Everything is structured around the PGDA syllabus and SAICA's competency requirements.

Do I have to buy a full module?

No. Sections are sold individually so you can buy only the topics you're weak on.

What are the bridging course notes?

I built them from my own studies on Milpark's advanced accounting bridging course to CTA, where I finished with an average over 70% and two distinctions. They follow that course, but they should help just as much if you're doing a bridging course to CTA at another university or institution. Switch the shop to "Bridging course" to see them.

Which syllabus do these follow?

The SAICA PGDA/CTA syllabus, with taxation based on current South African legislation and IFRS as applied in the local programme.

Can these notes replace the official course material?

No. They're a study aid, not a replacement for official SAICA/PGDA syllabus content. Every effort goes into making them accurate and thorough, but there's no guarantee they capture every concept, update or requirement in full.

Can I share the notes with my study group?
