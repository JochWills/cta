About

Notes I actually use to get through PGDA

Hi, I'm Courts. I am currently doing the same PGDA/CTA year you're doing now, after tutoring accounting all through my undergrad — so writing these notes is really an extension of that: taking a section and rebuilding it into something you could revise from in a single sitting.
