Privacy policy

Last updated 30 August 2026.

Short version: we collect what's needed to get your notes to you and keep a record of the sale, we don't sell or share it for marketing, and we don't run any tracking or analytics on this site.

1. Who this covers

This is the privacy policy for PGDA Notes by Courts, run by Courts as an individual seller. For anything in here, or a request about your own information, contact courtneywickens451@gmail.com — that's also the address to use for any request under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

2. What we collect

  • Email address — required, so your order can be matched back to you when you look up your download links.
  • Full name — optional, if you give it at checkout.
  • Order details — which sections you bought, the amount, and the order status.

We don't collect or see your card details at all. Payment is handled by Paystack, who process that directly — it never passes through our own systems.

3. Why we collect it

To match you to your order so you can look up the notes you paid for, keep a record of the sale for our own bookkeeping, and answer you if you get in touch with a question about an order. Paystack sends its own payment receipt separately, as the processor of the transaction.

4. Who it's shared with

Two service providers process data on our behalf to make the shop work:

  • Paystack — processes your payment. See their own privacy policy for how they handle card and payment data.
  • Supabase — hosts the database and file storage this site runs on.

Neither uses your information for their own marketing, and we don't sell or share your details with anyone else.

5. How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as reasonably needed for bookkeeping and to handle any questions about a past order.

6. Cookies and local storage

This site doesn't use tracking or analytics cookies. Your cart is saved in your browser's local storage purely so it survives a refresh, and the email you check out with is saved the same way so the "get your notes" screen can find your order automatically when you're sent back here after paying. Both stay on your device and are never sent anywhere on their own — the email is only sent back to us if you use it to look up an order, the same way typing it into that form would.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA, you can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (subject to what we're required to keep for legal or bookkeeping reasons, like tax records of a completed sale). Email courtneywickens451@gmail.com for any of this.

8. Keeping it safe

The site is served over HTTPS, and the database that holds order and product information uses row-level security so that, among other things, only server-side code — never the public website code — can read order details or mark an order paid.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time; the version on this page is the current one.
